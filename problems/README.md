Browse, filter and claim them on the lab website (page **Problems**). To work on one:

1. Open a **Project proposal** issue with the problem ID (the website's *Claim* button fills it in).
2. Create your repository from the CivilTech Innovation Starter and build the app.
3. Submit it with the website's **Submit a project** page.

Levels: Beginner (formulas, loops, arrays), Intermediate (classes, files, several methods), Advanced (algorithms, large datasets, numerical methods).

## Water resources and hydrology

### P001

**Design rainfall IDF curves for Vellore** (Intermediate)

- **Problem:** Engineers designing drains and culverts in Vellore need rainfall intensity for durations from 15 minutes to 24 hours and return periods of 2 to 100 years, but most use values copied from distant cities.
- **Who benefits:** Municipal engineers, drainage designers
- **Method:** Annual maximum daily rainfall series; Gumbel (EV1) frequency analysis by the method of moments; short-duration depths by the IMD empirical one-third power reduction P(t) = P24 (t/24)^(1/3).
- **Open data:** [IMD gridded rainfall 0.25 deg (daily, 1901-present)](https://www.imdpune.gov.in/); [CHIRPS daily rainfall 0.05 deg](https://www.chc.ucsb.edu/data/chirps); [Open-Meteo historical and forecast weather API (CC BY 4.0)](https://open-meteo.com/)
- **Java practised:** arrays, sorting, methods, Math functions
- **The app shows:** IDF table and log-log IDF curves, intensity for a chosen duration and return period, CSV export.
- **Stretch goal:** Compare Gumbel with log-Pearson III and show confidence intervals.

### P002

**Village tank storage by the sequent peak method** (Intermediate)

- **Problem:** Tamil Nadu's irrigation tanks (eris) silt up and fail in dry years. How much storage does a tank need to meet a given irrigation demand through a recorded dry spell?
- **Who benefits:** Water user associations, PWD engineers
- **Method:** Monthly inflow from catchment rainfall (runoff coefficient or SCS-CN) and the sequent peak algorithm (Thomas, 1963) for required storage; mass curve (Rippl) for comparison.
- **Open data:** [CHIRPS daily rainfall 0.05 deg](https://www.chc.ucsb.edu/data/chirps); [IMD gridded rainfall 0.25 deg (daily, 1901-present)](https://www.imdpune.gov.in/); [JRC Global Surface Water (1984-present)](https://global-surface-water.appspot.com/); [OpenStreetMap (ODbL), query with Overpass Turbo](https://overpass-turbo.eu/)
- **Java practised:** loops, arrays, classes
- **The app shows:** Inflow and demand charts, mass curve, required storage and reliability.
- **Stretch goal:** Add evaporation losses from NASA POWER and siltation scenarios.

### P003

**Drought monitor with the Standardized Precipitation Index** (Advanced)

- **Problem:** Districts of Tamil Nadu declare drought late. A simple SPI dashboard could flag developing drought months earlier.
- **Who benefits:** Agriculture department, students of climate risk
- **Method:** SPI (McKee, Doesken and Kleist, 1993): fit a gamma distribution to 3, 6 and 12-month rainfall totals and transform to a standard normal variate; drought classes from SPI thresholds.
- **Open data:** [CHIRPS daily rainfall 0.05 deg](https://www.chc.ucsb.edu/data/chirps); [IMD gridded rainfall 0.25 deg (daily, 1901-present)](https://www.imdpune.gov.in/)
- **Java practised:** arrays, methods, numerical functions, exceptions
- **The app shows:** SPI time series, current drought class map for selected grid points.
- **Stretch goal:** Compare SPI with soil moisture from ERA5-Land.

### P004

**Reference evapotranspiration calculator (FAO-56 Penman-Monteith)** (Beginner)

- **Problem:** Farmers and irrigation planners need daily crop water loss, but ET0 needs many weather variables and a long formula.
- **Who benefits:** Agricultural engineers, students
- **Method:** FAO-56 Penman-Monteith reference evapotranspiration (Allen et al., 1998), with the Hargreaves equation as a check when only temperature is available.
- **Open data:** [NASA POWER daily meteorology and solar radiation](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/); [Open-Meteo historical and forecast weather API (CC BY 4.0)](https://open-meteo.com/); [FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 56 (crop evapotranspiration)](https://www.fao.org/3/x0490e/x0490e00.htm)
- **Java practised:** variables, methods, Math functions, classes
- **The app shows:** Daily and monthly ET0 chart, comparison of methods, table of intermediate terms.
- **Stretch goal:** Add a crop coefficient curve to get crop evapotranspiration.

### P005

**Irrigation water requirement for paddy and sugarcane** (Intermediate)

- **Problem:** Vellore district grows paddy and sugarcane with groundwater. How much irrigation water does each crop need per season, after rainfall?
- **Who benefits:** Farmers, irrigation officers
- **Method:** Crop evapotranspiration ETc = Kc x ET0 with FAO-56 crop coefficients and growth stages; effective rainfall by the USDA SCS method used in FAO CROPWAT; field application efficiency.
- **Open data:** [NASA POWER daily meteorology and solar radiation](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/); [FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 56 (crop evapotranspiration)](https://www.fao.org/3/x0490e/x0490e00.htm); [CHIRPS daily rainfall 0.05 deg](https://www.chc.ucsb.edu/data/chirps)
- **Java practised:** arrays, loops, classes, enums
- **The app shows:** Ten-day irrigation schedule, seasonal volume per hectare, rainfall contribution.
- **Stretch goal:** Compare normal and drought-year seasons.

### P006

**Groundwater level trend analyser** (Intermediate)

- **Problem:** Groundwater levels in Vellore are falling in some wells and recovering in others. Which trends are statistically significant?
- **Who benefits:** CGWB analysts, local bodies
- **Method:** Mann-Kendall trend test and Sen's slope estimator on pre- and post-monsoon water levels.
- **Open data:** [India-WRIS water resources information system](https://indiawris.gov.in/); [Central Ground Water Board reports and manuals](https://cgwb.gov.in/)
- **Java practised:** ArrayList, sorting, methods
- **The app shows:** Map of wells coloured by trend direction and significance, hydrograph per well.
- **Stretch goal:** Relate trends to rainfall and land-use change.

### P007

**Pumping test analysis for a campus borewell** (Intermediate)

- **Problem:** Before installing a larger pump, the hostel needs the aquifer's transmissivity and storativity from a pumping test.
- **Who benefits:** Campus estate office, hydrogeology students
- **Method:** Cooper-Jacob straight-line method and Theis curve matching for drawdown versus time; safe yield estimate.
- **Open data:** Field or laboratory measurements collected by the team (publish them with the app); [Central Ground Water Board reports and manuals](https://cgwb.gov.in/)
- **Java practised:** arrays, linear regression, methods
- **The app shows:** Drawdown plot with fitted line, T and S values, predicted drawdown for a new pumping rate.
- **Stretch goal:** Add recovery test analysis (Theis recovery).

### P008

**Synthetic unit hydrographs from a DEM** (Advanced)

- **Problem:** Most small catchments in India are ungauged. How different are the Snyder and SCS synthetic unit hydrographs for the same catchment?
- **Who benefits:** Hydrology students, flood engineers
- **Method:** Catchment length, slope and area from the DEM; Snyder (1938) unit hydrograph and SCS dimensionless unit hydrograph (NEH-630 Ch. 16).
- **Open data:** [Copernicus GLO-30 DEM (AWS Open Data)](https://registry.opendata.aws/copernicus-dem/); [HydroSHEDS river networks and basins](https://www.hydrosheds.org/); [VIT Innovation Lab Vellore catchment dataset (in this repository)](https://github.com/parthibanlogan/JAVA/tree/main/site/data/catchment)
- **Java practised:** classes, interfaces, arrays
- **The app shows:** Overlaid unit hydrographs, peak and time-to-peak comparison, flood hydrograph for a design storm.
- **Stretch goal:** Add the Clark unit hydrograph and compare.

### P009

**Flood frequency analysis of annual peak discharges** (Intermediate)

- **Problem:** Bridge and levee design needs the 100-year flood. With a short discharge record, which distribution and confidence bands are defensible?
- **Who benefits:** Bridge designers, water resources engineers
- **Method:** Annual maximum series; Gumbel and log-Pearson type III (US Bulletin 17C) by moments; plotting positions (Weibull).
- **Open data:** [India-WRIS water resources information system](https://indiawris.gov.in/); [GloFAS global flood awareness system river discharge](https://global-flood.emergency.copernicus.eu/)
- **Java practised:** arrays, sorting, methods, exceptions
- **The app shows:** Probability plot, fitted curves, design floods for 10 to 500 years.
- **Stretch goal:** Show how adding recent extreme years changes the 100-year flood.

### P010

**Tank cascade water balance** (Advanced)

- **Problem:** Tanks in Tamil Nadu are linked in cascades: spill from one feeds the next. How does a heavy storm propagate down a cascade?
- **Who benefits:** Tank rehabilitation planners
- **Method:** Daily water balance per tank (inflow, rainfall, evaporation, irrigation draw, seepage, spill) with spill routed to the downstream tank; catchments from the DEM.
- **Open data:** [OpenStreetMap (ODbL), query with Overpass Turbo](https://overpass-turbo.eu/); [JRC Global Surface Water (1984-present)](https://global-surface-water.appspot.com/); [Copernicus GLO-30 DEM (AWS Open Data)](https://registry.opendata.aws/copernicus-dem/); [ERA5 hourly reanalysis (Copernicus Climate Data Store)](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/)
- **Java practised:** classes, objects linked in a list, loops
- **The app shows:** Cascade diagram, storage time series of every tank, spill events.
- **Stretch goal:** Simulate desilting or breach of one tank.

## Urban flooding and drainage

### P011

**Campus storm drain sizing** (Beginner)

- **Problem:** Several roads on campus flood after 40 mm/h rain. Are the existing drains big enough?
- **Who benefits:** Campus estate office
- **Method:** Rational method Q = C i A / 360 with Kirpich time of concentration and IDF intensity; drain capacity by Manning's equation (CPHEEO Manual on Storm Water Drainage Systems, 2019).
- **Open data:** [OpenStreetMap (ODbL), query with Overpass Turbo](https://overpass-turbo.eu/); [Copernicus GLO-30 DEM (AWS Open Data)](https://registry.opendata.aws/copernicus-dem/); [CPHEEO manuals (water supply, sewerage, storm water, solid waste)](https://cpheeo.gov.in/); Field or laboratory measurements collected by the team (publish them with the app)
- **Java practised:** variables, methods, classes
- **The app shows:** Table of drain segments with design flow, capacity and pass/fail; map of under-capacity drains.
- **Stretch goal:** Suggest the required drain size for every failing segment.

### P012

**Street flood hotspot mapper** (Intermediate)

- **Problem:** Which streets in Vellore sit in depressions where water ponds?
- **Who benefits:** Municipal corporation, residents
- **Method:** Depression depth = filled DEM minus original DEM (Priority-Flood); ponding volume per depression; intersect with OpenStreetMap roads.
- **Open data:** [Copernicus GLO-30 DEM (AWS Open Data)](https://registry.opendata.aws/copernicus-dem/); [OpenStreetMap (ODbL), query with Overpass Turbo](https://overpass-turbo.eu/); [VIT Innovation Lab Vellore catchment dataset (in this repository)](https://github.com/parthibanlogan/JAVA/tree/main/site/data/catchment)
- **Java practised:** 2-D arrays, priority queue, classes
- **The app shows:** Map of depressions ranked by volume and road length affected.
- **Stretch goal:** Use a finer DEM or survey levels for a single ward.

### P013

**Rooftop rainwater harvesting potential** (Beginner)

- **Problem:** How much rainwater could each hostel or house roof collect in a year, and how big should the tank be?
- **Who benefits:** Households, hostel wardens
- **Method:** Harvestable volume = rainfall x roof area x runoff coefficient (IS 15797:2008 guidelines); tank size by monthly mass balance.
- **Open data:** [Google Open Buildings footprints (CC BY 4.0 / ODbL)](https://sites.research.google/open-buildings/); [IMD gridded rainfall 0.25 deg (daily, 1901-present)](https://www.imdpune.gov.in/); [Open-Meteo historical and forecast weather API (CC BY 4.0)](https://open-meteo.com/)
- **Java practised:** variables, arrays, methods
- **The app shows:** Roof map with annual harvestable volume, monthly supply-demand chart, tank size.
- **Stretch goal:** Add first-flush diversion and cost per litre.

**Water demand projection for a city** (Beginner)

- **Problem:** How much water will Vellore need in 2041?

- **Method:** Population forecasting by arithmetic, geometric and incremental-increase methods (CPHEEO Manual on Water Supply and Treatment, 1999) and per-capita demand.
